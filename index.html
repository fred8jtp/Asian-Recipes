About Asian Meat Kitchen

A focused collection of authentic and popular Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Korean and Vietnamese recipes that use only chicken, pork or beef — no fish or seafood.

  • ~25 Chinese mains + classics
  • ~9 Japanese favorites
  • ~9 Thai dishes
  • ~9 Korean recipes
  • ~9 Vietnamese specialties
  • Large sides & appetizers section

Every recipe includes a full ingredient list, step-by-step directions, and practical substitution suggestions. The data is stored in a single JavaScript array so you can easily expand the collection later.

Favorites are saved in your browser (localStorage). This is a pure static site — no backend required. Host it on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or any static host.

Recipes are based on traditional methods and popular home-cook versions. Adjust spice and salt to taste.
